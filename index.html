WELCOME TO KMRS

A school designed for
learning and life.

Kalinga Model Residential Schools are Government of Odisha residential schools established to provide quality education and a supportive residential environment for eligible students.

KMRS Bissamcuttack is located in Rayagada district. The school website brings together practical information for students, parents, staff and visitors while maintaining a dedicated CBSE compliance area.
