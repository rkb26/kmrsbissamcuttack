WELCOME TO OUR SCHOOL

Education that goes
beyond the classroom.

Kalinga Model Residential Schools are Government of Odisha residential schools established to provide quality education and a supportive learning environment for eligible students.

KMRS Bissamcuttack is part of the State's KMRS network. The school combines academic learning with residential life, supervised study, activities, sports and opportunities for students to develop confidence and responsibility.
