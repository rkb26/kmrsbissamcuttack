ABOUT KMRS

Learning, living and growing together.

Kalinga Model Residential Schools were established by the Government of Odisha to provide quality education and residential schooling opportunities to ST, SC and PVTG students, following CBSE norms.

KMRS Bissamcuttack is listed by the Odisha Government among the KMRS schools in Rayagada district. The school is located at Bitaguda, Bissamcuttack.
