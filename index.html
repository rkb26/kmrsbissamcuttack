01 / ABOUT THE SCHOOL

A school built around opportunity.

Kalinga Model Residential Schools are Government of Odisha residential schools designed to provide quality education and a supportive residential environment for eligible students.

KMRS Bissamcuttack is located in Rayagada district and forms part of the State's KMRS network. The school's academic pathway is based on the CBSE course and NCERT syllabus, while residential life adds structured study, recreation, community living and personal development.

04 / ADMISSIONS

The journey to Class VI.

The 2026–27 KMRS selection framework provides admission to Class VI through the Kalinga Model Residential Schools Selection Test (KMRSST).

KMRSST 2026–27

Selection Test pattern

50Mental Ability

The official 2026–27 admission information also specifies eligibility and required documents. Always use the latest Government of Odisha notice for current dates and conditions.

Eligibility snapshot

  • Studying in Class V in an Odisha school
  • Eligible ST / SC / PVTG category
  • Age 10–13 years as on 31.03.2026 for 2026–27
  • Age relaxation provisions apply for differently abled children
