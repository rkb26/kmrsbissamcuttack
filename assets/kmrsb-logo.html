About KMRS Bissamcuttack

Transforming Tribal & Rural Education in Odisha

Kalinga Model Residential School (KMRS) Bissamcuttack is a premier co-educational residential institution managed under the aegis of the **ST & SC Development Department, Government of Odisha**.

Located in **Bitaguda, PO-Bissamcuttack (Rayagada District)**, the institution provides free education, boarding, nutritious meals, textbooks, and state-of-the-art facilities to deserving students from tribal and rural backgrounds.

Our Vision

To bridge the educational gap and nurture confident leaders for tomorrow.

Our Mission

All-round academic, physical, and moral development in a safe atmosphere.

Campus Facilities & Infrastructure

Comprehensive environment designed to foster academic excellence, physical health, and creative skills.

Residential Hostels

Secure, comfortable, and separate hostel facilities for boys and girls with round-the-clock supervision and medical support.

Computer & IT Labs

Modern computer labs equipped with high-speed internet and practical digital literacy instruction for all grades.

Science Laboratories

Fully equipped Physics, Chemistry, and Biology laboratories enabling hands-on learning and practical experimentation.

Library & Reading Room

Extensive collection of academic books, storybooks, reference guides, periodicals, and newspapers.

Sports & Playground

Large playground facilities for Football, Athletics, Volleyball, Badminton, and daily Yoga sessions.

Nutritious Mess Facilities

Hygienic dining hall serving balanced meals strictly adhering to government nutritional guidelines.
